The Nile — A Completely New Ecosystem

























































I wouldn't make the entire mod just sand. The contrast between the desert and the Nile would be beautiful.

Consider:
- Large winding rivers.
- Papyrus swamps.
- Crocodile-infested waters.
- Fishing villages.
- Ancient irrigation canals.
- Lotus ponds.
- Palm forests.
- Abandoned river ports.
- Small agricultural settlements.
This makes exploration much more varied.
2. The Living Pyramids

























































An idea I particularly like is having some pyramids function almost like enormous living dungeons.

Imagine:
- Stone statues that activate when you enter.
- Hieroglyphics that illuminate as you progress.
- Hidden passages that open when you solve puzzles.
- Giant rolling stone mechanisms.
- Rooms that fill with sand.
- Secret treasure chambers.
- A Pharaoh boss sitting on a throne.
Each pyramid could have a randomized layout and different guardians.
3. Pharaohs and Royal Dynasties













































Introduce different ancient pharaohs with individual identities.

Examples:
- The Sun Pharaoh — wielded solar magic.
- The Serpent Pharaoh — commanded poisonous creatures.
- The Forgotten Pharaoh — an undead ruler.
- The Golden Pharaoh — protected an enormous treasure vault.
- The Pharaoh of Shadows — ruled a hidden underground kingdom.
Each could have a unique tomb, armor set, royal weapon, and backstory.
4. Egyptian Mythological Mounts
Some particularly fun possibilities:
- Divine Falcon: A flying companion.
- Desert Camel: A practical desert mount with storage.
- War Chariot: A fast vehicle pulled by horses.
- Solar Lion: A powerful rare mount.
- Sacred Scarab: A rideable giant beetle.
- Serpent Mount: An extremely rare magical creature.
5. Make the Gods Actually Feel Present
One of my favorite ideas is occasional divine manifestations.

Rather than having the gods exist only as statues, players might occasionally encounter them.

For example:
- Ra appears as a massive glowing sun entity during an eclipse.
- Anubis appears outside a tomb to judge the player.
- Bastet occasionally appears as a mysterious black cat.
- Horus flies above the desert during thunderstorms.
- Thoth appears in ancient libraries.
These encounters could be extremely rare and memorable.
My biggest recommendation: Make the Egyptian mod and Asgard two completely independent projects, but establish a consistent design philosophy between them: divine shrines, legendary artifacts, unique creatures, ancient structures, and mythology-based progression.
