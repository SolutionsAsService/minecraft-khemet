Khemet
Kemet, “the Black Land”, is what the ancient Egyptians called their own country: the dark, fertile silt the Nile left each year. The desert beyond it was Deshret, “the Red Land”.
A Minecraft mod
An add-on that changes the game. This one adds new blocks, plants, potions, creatures and buildings.
A shrine and an altar
A shrine is a building for a god. Its altar is a block: you hold an offering (say, a gold ingot) and use it, and the god gives you a blessing.
Favor
A score each god keeps for you. More offerings, more favor, stronger blessings.

Divine favor

Each god takes offerings at its altar. Every offering adds favor, and favor moves you up four stages; each stage adds a blessing.

  1. 0Stranger
  2. 3Recognized
  3. 10Favored
  4. 25Chosen

The points are the thresholds in the mod’s own data file.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The mod loads on a Fabric server with no data errors.
  • All four structures generate on their own in a desert world, and the game’s /locate finds them.
  • Favor, the stage thresholds and the blessings work through the same code the altars use.
  • The sandstorm slows a player in the open and not under a roof.

Not yet tested:

  • Using an altar by hand (the same code path was tested through a command).
  • Brewing the potions.
  • The Ankh and the Khopesh in play.
  • A sandstorm starting on its own.
  • Mummies spawning on their own.
