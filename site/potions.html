Plants & potions

Lotus, papyrus and five brews

Two river plants and five divine potions, each set to be brewed from an Awkward Potion and one ingredient found in the desert structures. The recipes are registered; none has been brewed in play yet.

Plants

  • Blue Lotus

    A river flower that glows faintly (light level 3). Grows on sand, dirt, clay or sandstone. Craft two Lotus Columns from it and two white limestone.

  • Papyrus

    A tall reed with a feathered head, for river banks and oases. Grows on sand, dirt, clay or sandstone. A stand of papyrus turns up in the desert ruins’ chests.

The five potions

  • Blood of Ra

    Ra, Lord of the Sun. Strength I for 3:00. Fire Resistance I for 4:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Sunstone Powder. Found in Ra’s shrine, an obelisk field.

    Ground sunstone, still warm. Ra’s strength, and his protection from fire.

  • Tears of Isis

    Isis, Lady of Magic. Regeneration II for 0:45.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Sacred Myrrh. Found in Ra’s shrine, a desert ruin.

    Isis wept for Osiris, and where her tears fell, the river rose. Healing, slowly and then all at once.

  • Breath of Horus

    Horus, The Falcon. Speed II for 3:00. Slow Falling I for 2:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Falcon Feather. Found in Anubis’s shrine, an obelisk field.

    A feather from a falcon that flew too high to be seen. Speed, and a gentle landing.

  • Anubis’s Veil

    Anubis, Guide of the Dead. Invisibility I for 2:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Natron Salt. Found in Anubis’s shrine, a desert ruin.

    Natron dried the dead for seventy days. A pinch of it, and the living are as quiet as the dead.

  • Bastet’s Grace

    Bastet, Lady of the Cats. Luck II for 5:00. Speed I for 2:00.

    Brew an Awkward Potion with Golden Date. Found in Ra’s shrine, a desert ruin.

    Sweet as the temple cats’ favourite sin. Luck, and light feet.

The potions use the same data format as Asgard’s: one entry in potions.json adds an ingredient, a potion and a brewing recipe.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The five potions and their ingredient items load, with names and lore, and the recipes register.
  • The plants render with transparent backgrounds after a cutout fix, and are placed in the photograph.

Not yet tested:

  • Brewing, drinking or splashing the potions in play.
  • Planting the lotus and papyrus by hand (the placement rule is in the code).
