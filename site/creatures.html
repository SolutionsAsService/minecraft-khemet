Creatures

The wrapped guardians

The first two creatures of the design’s Phase 3. They use the zombie model with skins painted for the mod.

Mummy

A tomb guardian wrapped in linen with glowing amber eyes. As written in the code (not yet seen in a fight): slow and durable, its touch leaves a 10-second Weakness, and dry linen burns twice as hard, so fire does double damage. It does not burn in sunlight.

Mummy: facts
Stats28 health (14 hearts), 4 attack damage, 2 armour
SpawnsDeserts: 1 to 3 at a time, spawn weight 12. Set to guard the shrine of Anubis and the desert ruin (in the data; not yet watched).
DropsBone, String, Rotten Flesh, Natron Salt, Gold Nugget
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:mummy

Royal Mummy

An elite mummy from a royal burial, in a striped headdress, a collar of gold and turquoise and gold armour. More health, harder hits, and better drops, including a 4% chance at an Ankh (from the loot table).

Royal Mummy: facts
Stats60 health (30 hearts), 7 attack damage, 6 armour, knockback resistance 0.4
SpawnsDeserts: singly, spawn weight 1. Set to guard the shrine of Anubis (in the data; not yet watched).
DropsBone, String, Gold Nugget, Gold Ingot, Sunstone Powder, Sacred Myrrh, Ankh
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:royal_mummy

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Both load, render with their skins (wrapped linen, glowing eyes; the royal mummy in gold) and are summoned and photographed.
  • The stats are the ones in the code, read at build time.

Not yet tested:

  • Mummies spawning on their own over a night in a desert.
  • The Weakness touch and the double fire damage in a fight.
  • Their drops (the table is written, not yet watched).
  • Custom models and animations: they reuse the zombie’s.
