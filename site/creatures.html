Creatures

Linen, stone, shell and a crown

Eight creatures, each with its own model: built from many small moving parts, with a texture packed and painted by the mod’s generator, from a small data file (mobs/*.json).

Mummy

A tomb guardian wrapped in bands of linen, hunched with its arms out, with tattered ends streaming and one glowing amber eye in a gap. Its touch leaves Weakness, and dry linen burns twice as hard. It does not burn in sunlight.

Mummy: facts
Stats28 health (14 hearts), 4 attack damage, 2 armour, Weakness I for 10 s on hit
SpawnsDesert: 1 to 3 at a time, weight 12. Set to guard the shrine of Anubis and the desert ruin (in the data; not yet watched).
DropsBone, String, Rotten Flesh, Natron Salt, Gold Nugget
MeasuredIn a flat desert test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 7.0 s; first hit took 3 health on Easy; effects on the player: Weakness. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:mummy

Royal Mummy

An elite mummy from a royal burial in a striped nemes headdress with a cobra, a false beard, the broad collar of gold, turquoise and lapis, gold armbands and a short golden khopesh.

Royal Mummy: facts
Stats60 health (30 hearts), 7 attack damage, 6 armour, Weakness I for 10 s on hit
SpawnsDesert: 1 at a time, weight 1. Set to guard the shrine of Anubis (in the data; not yet watched).
DropsBone, String, Gold Nugget, Gold Ingot, Sunstone Powder, Sacred Myrrh, Ankh
MeasuredIn a flat desert test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 7.0 s; first hit took 4.5 health on Easy; effects on the player: Weakness. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:royal_mummy

A small sacred beetle with a turquoise shell striped in gold, red eyes, antennae and six legs in a tripod gait. Fast, fragile, and its bite poisons.

Crocodile

A Nile crocodile: a long armoured body with a ridge of scutes, splayed legs, a tapering tail of six segments, a long snout with teeth down both jaws and yellow eyes on raised bumps. It lurks around rivers, swamps and beaches and lunges.

The Sun Pharaoh

The sun king: bronze-skinned under a gold circlet and a halo of eight rays, in gold and red-orange armour with a flame-red kilt and streaming flames, a sun-gold khopesh and a sceptre topped with a red sun. A boss with a bar: it charges, stamps, enrages when hurt, and its hits blind you for a moment.

The Sun Pharaoh: facts
Stats260 health (130 hearts), 11 attack damage, 8 armour, Blindness I for 3 s on hit
SpawnsDesert: 1 at a time, weight 1.
DropsGold Ingot, Diamond, Sunstone Powder, Ankh

Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:sun_pharaoh

The Forgotten Pharaoh

The undead king: a golden death-mask under the double crown (white hedjet inside the red deshret), a nemes and uraeus, the broad collar, crook and flail crossed on the chest and a shroud of tattered linen. A boss with a bar: it hunts through walls, charges, slams, enrages below half health, and its blows leave Weakness II. It is not placed by anything yet.

The Forgotten Pharaoh: facts
Stats220 health (110 hearts), 9 attack damage, 8 armour, Weakness II for 8 s on hit
SpawnsNone: no structure or biome places it yet (an egg and /summon only).
DropsGold Ingot, Diamond, Sunstone Powder, Ankh
MeasuredIn a flat desert test world: reached a player 12 blocks away in 4.0 s; first hit took 5.5 health on Easy; effects on the player: Weakness. (One run each; shadw-mod playtest mobtest.)
Spawn eggIn the Spawn Eggs tab, and /summon khemet:forgotten_pharaoh

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • All eight load, render with their own models and are photographed. A test harness measured each in a running game: time to reach a player, the first hit, the effects, and its drops.
  • The mummy and royal mummy were moved from hand-written classes to data files and behave as before (Weakness on hit, as measured).

Not yet tested:

  • Mummies, scarabs and guardians spawning on their own over a night, and a real fight with any of them.
  • The Forgotten Pharaoh’s whole fight, and anywhere to find him: nothing places him yet.
  • How the models move in a fight (animation was checked only in still photographs).
