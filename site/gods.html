Gods

Ra and Anubis

The gods here are game material: a simplified retelling for a Minecraft mod, made with respect for the ancient Egyptian world it draws on, and not a statement about religion.

Each god has a shrine and an altar. Hold an offering, use the altar, and the god takes one item, adds to your favor, and gives you a blessing. Favor is saved with you, and kept through death.

Favor

  1. 0Stranger
  2. 3Recognized
  3. 10Favored
  4. 25Chosen

Use an altar with an empty hand to see your favor and what the god wants. /khemet favor lists your standing with every god.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Offering, refusal of the wrong item, favor kept per god, and all four stage names (Ra reached Chosen at 25 favor), through the command that runs the same code as the altar.
  • The blessings stack by stage: at Recognized, Ra gave Fire Resistance and Strength; at Favored, Regeneration and Resistance joined them; at Chosen (28 favor) Regeneration II, Fire Resistance and a longer Strength were active.
  • Both shrines generate on their own and are photographed.

Not yet tested:

  • Using an altar block with a held item (the click path).
  • Favor kept across a server restart (it is saved with the player; not yet restarted to check).
  • Anubis’s blessings and his stages beyond Stranger were not individually inspected.
