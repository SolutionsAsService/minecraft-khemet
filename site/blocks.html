Stone for the Black Land

Twelve building stones, two pillars and a brazier, all drawn for the mod. Every stone has a stonecutter recipe, from sandstone or a sibling stone (the cards say which). The recipes are written and checked by a linter, and not yet cut in play.

The twelve stones

  • Ancient Limestone

    The everyday wall block: pale, seamed, a little worn.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • White Egyptian Limestone

    Brighter limestone for temple faces and columns.

    Stonecutter, from Calcite

  • Golden Sandstone

    Banded gold sandstone for sun shrines and trim.

    Stonecutter, from Cut Sandstone

  • Black Basalt

    Dark flecked stone for the Black Land: tombs, and the jackal god’s court.

    Stonecutter, from Smooth Basalt

  • Polished Pharaoh Stone

    Smooth, even stone with a worn edge, for floors and plinths.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • Carved Hieroglyphic Stone

    Two rows of carved glyphs: ankh, wave, eye, bird, sun, reed, scarab.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • Cracked Temple Stone

    Limestone split by one long crack, for ruins.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • Weathered Pyramid Stone

    Mottled, eroded blocks for old pyramid faces.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • Gold-Inlaid Tile

    A pharaoh-stone tile with a gold cross inlaid.

    Stonecutter, from Polished Pharaoh Stone

  • Painted Temple Wall

    A painted band: gold, lapis and turquoise above, red bars below.

    Stonecutter, from Polished Pharaoh Stone

  • Hieroglyphic Bricks

    Glyphs on bricks, offset like a real wall.

    Stonecutter, from Sandstone

  • Obelisk Block

    Shaft stone with vertical inscriptions and gold ends.

    Stonecutter, from Polished Pharaoh Stone

Pillars and the brazier

  • Golden Pillar

    A pillar: place it upright or on its side. Gold bands and a gold ring on the end.

    Crafting: three Golden Sandstone in a column make three pillars

  • Lotus Column

    A white column with a turquoise lotus capital and a lapis band.

    Crafting: White Limestone above and below a Blue Lotus makes two columns

  • Sacred Brazier

    A bowl of fire in a black-and-gold stand. Light level 14, with flame and smoke.

    Crafting: Black Basalt across the bottom row and both sides of the middle row, Coal in the middle, a Gold Ingot in each top corner

The two altars (Ra and Anubis) are on the Gods page.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • Every block loads, shows its own texture, and its item shows in the inventory and tab.
  • The blocks in these photographs were placed in the game and photographed from a client.

Not yet tested:

  • Mining speeds and tool requirements.
  • The stonecutter and crafting recipes were checked by the generator’s linter, not by crafting them in play.
