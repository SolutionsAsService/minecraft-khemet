Structures

Four things in the sand

The desert hides two shrines, an obelisk field and a ruin. Each is a template with a few variants, generated by the game where the biome allows, with a chest of themed loot.

The shrine of Ra

A stepped limestone and golden-sandstone dais under a golden obelisk, with a gold-rimmed sun disc standing behind it, four golden pillars with braziers on top, and the altar of Ra in front.

The shrine of Ra: facts
Size21 by 21 blocks, 26 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 32 by 32 chunks
Biomesdesert
Guardiansnone
ChestSunstone Powder, Gold Ingot, Golden Apple, Golden Date, Sacred Myrrh, Sunflower, Lapis Lazuli, Potion of Blood of Ra, Ankh

The shrine of Anubis

A black-basalt court with a gold trim: two jackal statues in gold collars, four canopic jars, a sarcophagus of dark slabs, soul campfires at the corners, and the altar of Anubis. Mummies guard it.

The shrine of Anubis: facts
Size19 by 19 blocks, 14 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 34 by 34 chunks
Biomesdesert, badlands
GuardiansMummy (2 to 3), Royal Mummy (1). Set in the structure’s data; not yet watched in play.
ChestNatron Salt, Bone, Gold Ingot, Soul Sand, Falcon Feather, Lapis Lazuli, Potion of Anubis’s Veil, Khopesh

The obelisk field

Five obelisks of different heights on a pharaoh-stone floor drifted with sand: slender shafts of carved stone banded in gold, each capped with a golden pyramidion that gives light, and a small chest.

The obelisk field: facts
Size25 by 25 blocks, 18 high; 2 variants
SpacingOne in every 30 by 30 chunks
Biomesdesert
Guardiansnone
ChestGold Nugget, Lapis Lazuli, Hieroglyph Stone, Falcon Feather, Golden Sandstone, Sunstone Powder

The desert ruin

A broken temple court: walls of weathered and cracked stone worn to different heights, lotus columns and golden pillars (some fallen), a painted wall of glyphs, a chest, and sand drifted in. Mummies haunt it.

The desert ruin: facts
Size15 by 15 blocks, 10 high; 3 variants
SpacingOne in every 20 by 20 chunks
Biomesdesert
GuardiansMummy (1 to 2). Set in the structure’s data; not yet watched in play.
ChestGold Nugget, Natron Salt, Golden Date, Bone, Papyrus, Emerald, Sacred Myrrh

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • All four generate on their own in a desert world: the game’s /locate found each one, from 16 to 417 blocks away.
  • All four were also placed with the template command and photographed from a client.

Not yet tested:

  • How they sit among vanilla desert structures (a flat test world has none).
  • Pyramids, the Sphinx and temples are not in this slice.
