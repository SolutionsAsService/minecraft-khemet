Relics & weather

An ankh, a khopesh, and a storm

Relics

  • Ankh of Life

    Use it to heal 4 hearts at once and then regenerate (II, 10 seconds). It also clears poison and wither. It wears out after 32 uses and needs 30 seconds between uses. It does nothing at full health.

    Shapeless: Gold Ingot ×2, Sunstone Powder, Sacred Myrrh. Also found in Ra’s shrine, and rarely dropped by royal mummies.

  • Anubis’s Khopesh

    A curved sword: +5 attack damage (6 with a bare hand’s 1) at 1.8 swings per second, 800 durability, and 4 extra magic damage to the undead.

    Crafting: GG·/··G·/··S; G = Gold Ingot, S = Stick. Also found in Anubis’s shrine.

Sandstorms

In daytime, in clear weather, there is a 1 in 90 chance every 10 seconds (about once every 15 minutes) that a sandstorm starts if any player is in a desert. It lasts 2:30. Anyone in a desert or badlands under open sky is slowed (Slowness I) and blown with sand; stand under a roof and nothing happens. Operators can start and stop one with /khemet sandstorm start and stop.

Status

Seen in a running Minecraft 1.21.4 game:

  • The Ankh and the Khopesh load, show their icons, and are photographed at display size.
  • A sandstorm applied Slowness to a player in the open, and none under a roof (measured on the player’s effects).

Not yet tested:

  • Using the Ankh, and the Khopesh’s undead bonus, in play.
  • A sandstorm starting on its own (it has been started with the command).
  • Sandstorm visuals: the particles are sparse, as the photograph shows, and the sky does not darken.
